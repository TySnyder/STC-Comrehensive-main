# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Admin staff at a behavioral health facility. They use this portal throughout their shift to manage the operational and clinical side of patient care — not clinicians providing direct therapy, but the people keeping the facility running: tracking who's here, who's leaving, who needs intervention, and what's overdue.

## Product Purpose

STC Operations Portal is an internal clinical operations platform for behavioral health staff. It centralizes client management, attendance and census tracking, discharge planning, admissions, staff management, clinical analytics and reporting, and settings. The architecture is explicitly extensible — many more modules will be added as the facility's operational needs grow. Success means staff can complete their core daily operational tasks without switching tools, and supervisors have the visibility they need to catch issues before they escalate.

## Brand Personality

Trusted. Precise. Efficient.

The portal should feel like a tool built specifically for healthcare operations: not sterile or cold, but calm and serious. Staff are managing real people in vulnerable situations — the interface should never feel noisy, gamified, or consumer-grade. It earns trust through clarity and reliability.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (Notion, Linear, Airtable vibes) — too casual and product-forward for clinical context
- Consumer health apps (MyFitnessPal, Headspace) — wrong register entirely
- Overdesigned analytics tools (Tableau-style) — data-heavy but not operationally useful
- Government/legacy EHR systems (Epic, PointClickCare) — institutional but visually hostile and slow-feeling

## Design Principles

1. **Operational clarity over visual novelty** — every design decision should reduce cognitive load for staff mid-shift, not impress at first glance.
2. **Dense but not cluttered** — behavioral health admin workflows involve a lot of data. The UI should pack information efficiently without making the screen feel overwhelming.
3. **Trust through consistency** — the same interaction pattern should always do the same thing. Predictability is a feature in high-stakes environments.
4. **Extensible by design** — new modules should feel like they were always there. The nav and layout patterns must scale to many more pages without feeling bolted on.
5. **Calm urgency** — alert states and risk flags must be visually distinct and attention-grabbing, but the baseline UI should stay quiet so alerts actually stand out.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High-contrast text on all interactive elements. Reduced-motion alternatives for any transitions. No color-only information encoding for risk or status indicators — always pair color with label or icon.
